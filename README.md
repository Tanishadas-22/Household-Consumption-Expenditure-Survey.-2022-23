As per the administrative changes, the following districts were newly created:

Shamli, created from Muzaffarnagar

Sambhal, created   from Moradabad

Hapur, created  from Ghaziabad

Amethi, formed by combining parts of RaeBareli and Sultanpur

For Shamli, Sambhal, and Hapur, as they have been created entirely from a single existing district, the  of MPCE estimates, along with their associated Relative Standard Errors (RSEs) and variance estimates, have been considered the same as those of their respective parent districts.

Estimation for Amethi District:

Amethi has been formed by combining parts of RaeBareli and Sultanpur. Hence, its estimates are derived using a population-weighted approach, based on Census 2011 population figures.


MPCE Estimate for Amethi:

((w1* Rae Bareli’s MPCE)+ (w2* Sultanpur’s MPCE)) / (w1+w2)


Variance Estimate for Amethi:

(w1^2 *  Variance of Rae Bareli’s MPCE estimate) + (w2^2 *  Variance of Sultanpur’s MPCE estimate) / (w1+w2)^2

Where:


w1 = Population of Rae Bareli (2011 Census)

w2 = Population of Sultanpur (2011 Census)
